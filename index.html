🎓 What is IELTS? — Complete Beginner to Advanced Guide
IELTS (International English Language Testing System) is the world's most popular English proficiency test, accepted by 11,000+ organizations in 140+ countries including universities in UK, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand. It tests 4 skills: Reading, Writing, Listening, and Speaking — each scored 0–9.

Master basic grammar, 500 core IELTS words, understand test format. Practice reading simple passages daily.

💡 Top 15 IELTS Secrets — Band 7+ Strategies
📖 Reading
Always skim the passage FIRST. Use 2–3 mins to understand the structure before reading questions.
📖 Reading
For True/False/Not Given — 'Not Given' means the info is simply NOT in the text, not that it's false.
🎧 Listening
Read the questions BEFORE the audio plays. Predict what kind of answer you need (name, number, place).
🎧 Listening
Spelling counts! If you write "recieve" instead of "receive", you lose the mark even if the word is right.
✍️ Writing
Always start Task 1 with "The graph/chart illustrates..." — never copy the question word for word.
✍️ Writing
Task 2 is worth MORE marks. Spend 40 minutes on it, only 20 minutes on Task 1.
✍️ Writing
Spend 5 minutes PLANNING your essay. A clear plan = structured essay = higher band score.
🗣️ Speaking
Never give one-word answers in Part 1. Always explain WHY using "because", "so", "which means".
🗣️ Speaking
In Part 2, use the 1-minute preparation time wisely. Write 4 bullet points on the cue card before speaking.
🏆 General
Write the full test under real exam conditions at least TWICE before your actual test date.
🏆 General
If you don't know a word, paraphrase it. Never leave a gap — always write something meaningful.
🏆 General
Band 7 requires: Flexible vocabulary, complex grammar, clear structure, and fully relevant ideas.
